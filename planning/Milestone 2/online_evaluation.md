# Online evaluation

**Accuracy metric and telemetry.** Our monitoring implementation measures the **positive-rating fraction following recommendations**. It consumes PostgreSQL `stream_events` (user/movie IDs, rating and event time) and `recommendation_serves` (user, ranked movie IDs, timestamp and model version). A rating is attributed when the same user received that movie in any recommendation list during the preceding 24 hours, including the rating timestamp. Each rating event counts once even if multiple lists match; repeated rating events remain separate observations. With the default positive threshold of **6/10**, accuracy is `positive attributed ratings / all attributed ratings`. Attribution coverage is `attributed ratings / all evaluated ratings`; a zero denominator produces an unavailable value, not zero accuracy. This is an observational satisfaction proxy, not classification accuracy or evidence that recommendations caused satisfaction. Users who do not rate are absent from its denominator. See [attribution](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/database/interactions/rating_attributions.py) and [accuracy calculation](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/monitoring/calculations/live_model_accuracy.py).

**Cost metrics.** Licensing cost sums known charges in `licensing_costs` by charge timestamp. The [ingestion rule](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/database/interactions/stream_events.py) creates a charge for a user's first recorded watch of a movie, or a watch at least 24 hours after their previous recorded watch of that movie; minute requests are therefore not each treated as paid plays. This is a billing proxy. LLM cost sums recorded `estimated_cost_usd` in `llm_usage`, alongside input/output tokens and request counts. Both calculators report priced versus total records: missing prices do not establish free usage. These are recorded estimates, not invoices or measurements of VM operating cost. See [cost calculators](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/tree/main/src/monitoring/calculations).

**Operationalization and system quality.** The [worker](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/monitoring/workload.py) computes completed, nonoverlapping five-minute UTC buckets, writes them idempotently to `monitoring_runs`, revisits the preceding hour for late arrivals, and fills missing buckets within 24 hours. Older late arrivals require explicit reprocessing. [Grafana](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/monitoring/grafana/dashboards/monitoring-overview.json) displays rolling 24-hour costs and accuracy calculated from summed counts, not averaged percentages. Successful-run freshness and logged monitoring failures indicate monitoring health, not recommendation-service availability. Missing buckets or ingestion must be checked before interpreting totals.

**Model comparison and results.** Daily candidate comparison separately uses held-out NDCG@20/Recall@20 with ratings **>=7**, storing comparison evidence in `model_logs`; those offline scores are not the online metric above. Online buckets can mix model versions and do not establish a causal candidate-versus-current comparison. See [daily evaluation](https://github.com/jwatson8/The-Neural-Network/blob/main/src/model_updates/evaluation.py). **Production results are not yet available for this report.** The [evidence and collection instructions](online_evaluation_results.md) distinguish verified calculator tests from the live measurements still required before submission.
